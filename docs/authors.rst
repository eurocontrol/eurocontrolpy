eurocontrolpy was developed at the
`Aviation Intelligence Unit <https://www.eurocontrol.int>`_ of
`EUROCONTROL <https://www.eurocontrol.int>`_.

       | ✉ `enrico.spinielli@eurocontrol.int <mailto:enrico.spinielli@eurocontrol.int>`_

Both authors are members of the `Performance Review Unit (PRU)
<https://www.eurocontrol.int/air-navigation-services-performance-review>`_ at EUROCONTROL.
